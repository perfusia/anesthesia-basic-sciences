Q1
An airway pressure of 27 cmH2O expressed in mmHg is approximately:
MechanismDivide by 1.36. 27 / 1.36 = 19.9 mmHg. The ratio comes from the density difference between mercury and water.
Q2
A substance below its critical temperature and coexisting as liquid and gas is correctly termed a:
MechanismA vapour is the gaseous phase of a substance below its critical temperature, and can therefore be liquefied by pressure alone. Above the critical temperature it is a gas and cannot.
Q3
Oxygen cannot be liquefied at room temperature because its critical temperature is:
MechanismRoom temperature sits far above −118 °C, so no applied pressure produces a liquid phase. Nitrous, at 36.5 °C, is the opposite case.
Q4
A nitrous E-cylinder gauge reads 745 psi. Remaining contents are:
MechanismWhile liquid remains, the gauge reads the saturated vapour pressure of nitrous at that temperature, which is independent of quantity. Only weighing gives contents.
Q5
The tropical filling ratio for nitrous oxide cylinders is:
Mechanism0.67 in tropical climates against 0.75 in temperate ones. Higher ambient temperature expands the liquid more, so more vapour headspace is required.
Q6
An oxygen cylinder reads 1600 psi at 15 °C. At 40 °C the reading becomes approximately:
Mechanism288 K → 313 K. 1600 × 313/288 = 1739 psi. Using Celsius produces the 4267 distractor and is the standard error.
Q7
Which law relates cylinder pressure to absolute temperature at fixed volume?
MechanismGay-Lussac holds volume constant, which is exactly the condition inside a rigid cylinder. Boyle holds temperature constant and Charles holds pressure constant.
Q8
A patient breathes 30% oxygen at a barometric pressure of 700 mmHg. Inspired oxygen tension is:
Mechanism0.30 × (700 − 47) = 196 mmHg. The 210 distractor omits the water vapour correction.
Q9
Diffusion of anesthetic across the alveolar membrane is driven by a gradient of:
MechanismPartial pressure is the driving force for all gas movement. Percentage only predicts behaviour correctly when total pressure is held fixed.
Q10
At high altitude, a variable bypass isoflurane vaporiser delivers:
MechanismSaturated vapour pressure depends on temperature alone, so the same absolute vapour quantity is added. Against reduced ambient pressure this is a higher fraction, leaving partial pressure roughly preserved.
Q11
The desflurane vaporiser at altitude under-doses because it:
MechanismIt is an electronically controlled gas blender delivering a target percentage. Percentage of a lower total pressure is a lower partial pressure, and unlike a variable bypass design there is no self-correction.
Q12
A nitrous cylinder contains 2.8 kg of nitrous oxide. Deliverable volume is approximately:
Mechanism(2800 / 44) × 22.4 = 63.6 mol × 22.4 = 1425 L.
Q13
Rapid opening of a cylinder valve risks ignition because of:
MechanismSudden pressurisation of the dead space compresses that gas faster than heat can escape, and temperatures can reach several hundred degrees, igniting hydrocarbon contamination in an oxygen-rich environment.
Q14
The Joule-Thomson effect requires:
MechanismThe temperature change arises from energy consumed overcoming intermolecular attraction during expansion. An ideal gas, by definition, has none, so it would show no effect.
Q15
A cylinder gauge reading zero means the cylinder is at absolute vacuum.
MechanismGauges read relative to ambient. Zero gauge means the contents sit at atmospheric pressure, so a full cylinder volume of gas remains.
Q16
A separated Entonox cylinder delivers oxygen-rich gas before hypoxic gas.
MechanismNitrous liquefies at the bottom, leaving an oxygen-enriched gas phase above that is drawn off first. The hypoxic nitrous-rich fraction comes at the end, which is what makes it dangerous.
Q17
Water vapour pressure in the airway falls proportionally as barometric pressure falls.
MechanismSaturated vapour pressure depends only on temperature. At 37 °C it stays at 47 mmHg regardless of altitude, so it consumes a growing proportion of total pressure as you ascend.
Q18
Pipeline gas supply pressure, in psi.
MechanismAround 50–55 psi. Cylinder regulators are deliberately set slightly lower, near 45 psi, so the pipeline is used preferentially when both are available.
Q19
Saturated water vapour pressure at 37 °C, in mmHg.
Mechanism47 mmHg, temperature-dependent and pressure-independent. It is subtracted from barometric pressure before applying any fractional concentration.
Q20
Name the gas law: at constant temperature, pressure varies inversely with volume.
MechanismBoyle's law. It underpins cylinder contents estimation and body plethysmography, both of which trade pressure against volume at fixed temperature.
