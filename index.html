Anesthesia Basic Sciences

The basic-science core underneath nurse anesthesia practice, built to be worked module by module. Mechanism first, name second, derivation before formula.

The COA requires a minimum of 30 graduate credits across anatomy, physiology, pathophysiology, pharmacology, chemistry, biochemistry, and physics. This site covers the two slices of that requirement with no prerequisite anywhere in a nursing pathway.

Every page is teach-then-test. Self-checks lock on first answer, show the mechanism on lock for right and wrong alike, and Copy my misses exports question, your answer, correct answer, and mechanism.
