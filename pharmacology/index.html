Track II · Pharmacokinetics & Pharmacodynamics

Compartment models as the coupled differential equations they actually are, and the dose-response layer sitting on top of them. Depends on Module 2 and on calculus, and on nothing else in Track I.

M7Pharmacokinetics I — Single Compartmentpending

Volume of distribution and clearance as the two independent variables, extraction ratio, first and zero order kinetics, half-life as a derived quantity, protein binding, pKa and ion trapping.

M8Pharmacokinetics II — Multicompartment and Infusionpending

Two and three compartment models, alpha beta gamma phases as eigenvalues of the rate matrix, redistribution against elimination, context-sensitive half-time defined properly, effect site and ke0, hysteresis, TCI.

M9Pharmacodynamics and Inhalational Uptakepending

The capstone. Dose-response, potency against efficacy, receptor theory, MAC and its modifiers, the FA/FI curve where gas laws, partition coefficients, flow, and kinetics all converge on one graph.
